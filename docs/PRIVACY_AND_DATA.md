# Privacy and Local Clipboard Data

KOPYAHON is designed to keep clipboard history on the local computer. Clipboard contents can still be sensitive, so users should understand what may be recorded and how to protect the local database.

## What to keep in mind

- Clipboard history may contain copied messages, addresses, code, or other private text.
- Avoid copying passwords, recovery codes, or payment details while history recording is enabled.
- Use the application's recording controls and exclusions, where available, for sensitive workflows.
- Protect your operating-system account with a strong password and screen lock.
- Be mindful when backing up or moving the local SQLite database; a backup may contain the same private clipboard entries.

## Shared computers

Do not leave clipboard history accessible on a shared or public computer. Clear sensitive entries and close the application before handing the device to someone else.

## Data handling

Local storage reduces exposure to remote services, but it does not protect data from other users or malware that can access your account files. Review the project's current settings and implementation to understand the available retention and deletion controls.
